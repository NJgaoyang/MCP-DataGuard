## UI/UX Design Guidance

- Avoid unnecessary secondary labels and descriptions.
- Let the layout do the explaining.

When building or revising the MCP-DataGuard UI, use clear hierarchy, spacing, grouping, and recognizable controls to communicate meaning. Keep text that is essential for security, accessibility, validation, approvals, permissions, or irreversible actions; do not remove important context merely to make the interface minimal.
